What to do with it

The point is not the minutes. It is noticing that "a day of coding" was actually an hour and a half of reading, then coding, then a short review. A week of these logs is enough to see which kind of work keeps getting pushed to "later".

The honest limit

Themes come from a short word list, so "read the notes" lands under learning and "write the readme" under writing. If a line is ambiguous, the first matching theme wins. That is a guess, and it is printed so you can disagree with it.
