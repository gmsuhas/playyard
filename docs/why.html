Why playyard

small on purpose

Who it is for

Someone who likes to understand a thing by making a small version of it. Not a team rollout, not a platform. One person, one evening, one file you can read from top to bottom.

How it helps

Forks pile up because cloning is easy and finishing is not. Playyard is the opposite move: take one idea from something you saved, cut it down until it fits in a single file, and keep the tests next to it. You end up with something you can run, explain, and change.

  • bhai makes a programming language concrete. Print, assign, add, loop.
  • digest turns a messy day into a few themes, so you can see what you actually did.
  • page publishes either of those as one HTML file. No install, no build, no account.

What I left out on purpose

No package manager, no web framework, no nested expressions, no functions in the toy language. Every one of those would make it look more serious and teach less. The constraint is the feature.

Try it in three commands

python3 -m playyard bhai   examples/hello.bhai
python3 -m playyard digest examples/day.log
python3 -m playyard page "Counting in bhai" examples/counting.txt "one loop, then done"
