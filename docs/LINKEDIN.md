I keep forking interesting repos and then never opening them again. This week I
did the opposite with three of them.

Playyard is a handful of text tools in dependency-free Python. Each one is a
small version of an idea I had saved:

- a toy programming language, cut down to print, assign, add, and one loop
- a day log that adds up where the time actually went
- a page builder that emits one HTML file, styling included, with nothing to install

The constraint is the point. One file you can read top to bottom teaches more
than a framework you only configure, and a week of plain-text logs shows the
work that keeps getting pushed to "later".

Who it's for: anyone who would rather understand a thing by making a small
version of it.

https://github.com/gmsuhas/playyard

Where Saturday went: https://gmsuhas.github.io/playyard/saturday.html
